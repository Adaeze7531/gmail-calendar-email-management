# Email, Calendar & Customer Support Management

A practical Virtual Assistant project demonstrating email management, calendar coordination, customer support, follow-ups, and professional communication.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I approach common day-to-day administrative support tasks involving email, calendars, and customer communication.

The focus is on keeping communication organized, responding to requests professionally, managing scheduled activities, and making sure important follow-ups are not missed.

## What I Did

### Email Management

I created a simple process for reviewing and organizing incoming emails. This includes identifying messages that require action, prioritizing important requests, organizing the inbox, responding to messages, and flagging items that require follow-up.

### Calendar Management

I demonstrated a structured approach to managing calendar information by reviewing scheduled events, confirming details, organizing appointments, checking for potential conflicts, and updating events when necessary.

### Customer Support

I practiced responding to common customer requests, including refund inquiries and access or download issues. The focus was on understanding the customer's request, providing a clear and professional response, and identifying when additional follow-up or escalation may be required.

### Follow-Up & Communication

I also included a follow-up process for outstanding requests to help ensure that important conversations do not get overlooked.
